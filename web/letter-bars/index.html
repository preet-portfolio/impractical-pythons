How the rock is made

The Python reads your words one character at a time and drops each letter into its own jar, counting as it goes. Accents get a gentle wash first, so é joins the e jar and ñ the n jar, and the receipt lists every accent that was folded in.

Each stick shows a letter's share of all your letters, so a postcard and a novel can stand side by side. The chocolate notches mark ordinary English. Try the Spanish postcard: A and O shoot up, while H, W and Y almost vanish. Every language has its own flavour. Peek at the Python.

Inspired by the letter bar chart project in Impractical Python Projects by Lee Vaughan, a great book if you want to make your own silly, clever programs. This version, its words and its code are new. One of the stalls on the Impractical Pythons pier.
