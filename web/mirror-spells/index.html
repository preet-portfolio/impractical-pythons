      How the mirror decides

      The mirror writes your word backwards underneath and checks every letter against the one above it. If they all match, it's a spell. Spaces, capitals and commas don't count, so whole sentences can be spells too: try Was it a car or a cat I saw?

      In Python, writing a word backwards is a tiny trick: word[::-1], a slice that walks the word one step at a time in reverse. For the spellbook the same check runs on all 60,388 words of a free dictionary, right here in your browser. Peek at the Python.

      Inspired by the palindrome project in Impractical Python Projects by Lee Vaughan, a great book if you want to make your own silly, clever programs.
