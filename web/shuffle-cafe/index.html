        How the board works

        Two words are anagrams when they use the same letters, the same number of times. The trick is to put each word's letters in alphabetical order: listen and silent both become eilnst. Same sorted letters, same family.

        The café does that sorting for all 60,388 words of a free dictionary once, when it opens, and files each word under its sorted letters. After that, finding your name's anagrams is a single look in the right drawer, all in Python, right here in your browser. Peek at the Python.

        Inspired by the anagram project in Impractical Python Projects by Lee Vaughan, a great book if you want to make your own silly, clever programs. Words from Alan Beale's public-domain 12dicts lists. This version, its words and its code are new. One of the stalls on the Impractical Pythons pier.
