    The rules of the room

    Grab the consonants at the front of a word, move them to the back, and add ay: nix becomes ix-nay. Words that start with a vowel just get the ay: apple-ay. A qu sticks together like a good partner (queen is een-quay).

    Plain old Pig Latin has a weakness: does away mean way or a? Code-Talk's little hyphen marks the snip, so the trip back is never a guess. Capitals, numbers and punctuation keep their places. Curious how a few lines of Python pull this off? Peek at the Python.
