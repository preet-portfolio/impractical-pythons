    How the machine pays out

    Each reel is a long strip of words, and every pull stops them somewhere new. Love one? Hold it and spin the others. Now and then a wildcard middle name sneaks in between the first and last names, just for fun.

    Three names starting with the same letter is the jackpot. Two that rhyme, or two that share a first letter, win smaller prizes. The machine works out your exact odds by trying every possible spin, all several thousand of them, in a blink. Each alias also gets a short code, so you can send a friend the exact one you landed. Peek at the Python.
