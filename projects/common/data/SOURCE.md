# Where the word list comes from

`2of4brif.txt` is from **12dicts** version 6.0.2 by Alan Beale
(<http://wordlist.aspell.net/12dicts/>, file `International/2of4brif.txt` in
`12dicts-6.0.2.zip`). It holds 60,388 common English words: the ones that appear in at least
two of four British and international learner's dictionaries.

From the 12dicts ReadMe: Alan Beale explicitly releases the 12dicts lists to the public
domain and asks for acknowledgment of their use. Thank you, Alan.

The only change made here: Windows line endings (`\r\n`) were turned into plain newlines.
