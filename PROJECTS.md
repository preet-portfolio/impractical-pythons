# Projects

| # | Project | What it does | What it teaches |
|---|---------|--------------|-----------------|
| 01 | [Alias Slots](projects/01_alias_slots/) | A fruit machine for ridiculous aliases: five themed machines, hold the reels you like, jackpots for alliteration, a share link for every name. | Tuples, `random.Random(seed)`, frozen dataclasses, `itertools.product` to count odds, mixed-radix base-36 codes, `argparse`, printing to `stderr`, PEP 8/257 with ruff |
| 02 | [Code-Talk](projects/02_code_talk/) | Pig Latin with a secret handshake: a hyphen marks each snip, so every message turns back exactly. | String slicing, regular expressions, `re.sub` with a function, round-trip testing |
| 03 | [Letter Bars](projects/03_letter_bars/) | A letter-frequency chart for any text, all 26 letters, side by side with typical English or another language. | `collections.defaultdict`, `unicodedata` accent folding, sorting with a key, `pprint` |
| 04 | [Mirror Spells](projects/04_mirror_spells/) | A magic mirror that checks whether a word or phrase reads the same both ways, a spellbook of every palindrome in a 60,000-word dictionary, 2,230 two-word spells, and a race between a list and a set. | Reverse slicing `[::-1]`, `try`/`except` with `sys.exit(1)`, sets and hashing, `cProfile`/`pstats`, `time.perf_counter`, recursion |
| 05 | [Shuffle Café](projects/05_shuffle_cafe/) | A café letterboard that re-pegs your name into its anagrams, a menu of the dictionary's biggest anagram families, and a phrase builder that turns a name into words one pick at a time, a chef who invents whole phrases, and a kitchen sieve that strains shuffled letters into new, sayable words. | `sorted()` as a fingerprint, a dict as an index, `defaultdict`, `collections.Counter` subtraction, `itertools.permutations`, `math.factorial`, n-gram counting, filtering in stages, depth-first search with a budget, tuple sort keys, `input()` with swappable `ask`/`say`, shared modules |
| 06 | [Pier Telegraph](projects/06_pier_telegraph/) | A telegraph office that files your message into a pigeonhole rack and wires it out along a secret route, with a code book, filler words, a new telegram to crack every day, challenge links to send a friend, a night clerk who brute-forces every route with your hunches, and a rail fence on the promenade. | Route ciphers, `route_order` with signed keys, a list-of-lists matrix, `list.pop()`, `[::-1]`, custom exception classes, `raise ... from None`, `for`/`else`, `sys.exit(1)` and `stderr`, a settings block, seeded daily puzzles, `hashlib` fingerprints, `json` + `base64` links, brute force with `itertools.permutations`/`product`, the rail fence with `zip_longest`, dynamic programming, chi-squared |
| 07 | [The Postcard Rack](projects/07_postcard_rack/) | A rack of seaside postcards, some hiding a second message between the lines: turn the reading glass until the hidden letters light up, let it try every setting, or test any text of your own. | Null ciphers and steganography, `string.punctuation`, a reset counter with a `found_first` flag, returning positions instead of values, a dict of functions, `re.finditer`, scoring with coverage and word length, shared code in `common/` |
